LOWER MARKET EXPOSURE

Fixed deposits

Offer a stated interest rate for a chosen term. Check the interest rate, premature-withdrawal conditions, taxation, and applicable deposit protection.

MARKET-LINKED

Mutual funds

Pool investors' money into assets such as bonds or shares. Risk, fees, and potential returns vary by fund. Returns are not guaranteed.

LONG-TERM PLANNING

Diversified equity investments

May suit some long-term plans, but market values can fall substantially. Consider diversification, your time horizon, and your ability to tolerate losses.
